# LAB 3.3 – iOS Device Enrollment

## Goal

The goal of this lab is to **enroll an iOS device into Microsoft Intune** in order to manage and secure mobile devices within the organization.

This enables centralized management and enforcement of security policies on iOS devices.

---

## Why This Matters

Mobile devices often access corporate data but are frequently unmanaged.

Without proper iOS enrollment:
- Devices cannot be evaluated for compliance
- Security policies cannot be enforced
- Corporate data is exposed on personal devices

iOS enrollment ensures **visibility, control, and security** for mobile endpoints.

---

## What Was Implemented

During this lab, the following configuration and actions were performed:

- Configuration of Apple Push Notification service (APNs)
- Linking Apple services with Microsoft Intune
- Installation of the Company Portal app
- Enrollment of an iOS test device
- Verification of device management and ownership

The device was successfully registered as **Intune-managed**.
