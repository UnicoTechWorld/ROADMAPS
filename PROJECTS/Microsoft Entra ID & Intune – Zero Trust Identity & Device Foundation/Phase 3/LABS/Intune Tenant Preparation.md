# LAB 3.1 – Intune Tenant Preparation

## Goal

The goal of this lab is to **prepare the Microsoft Intune tenant** so that devices can be enrolled, managed, and secured in a controlled and structured way.

This lab establishes the **device management foundation** for all subsequent Phase 3 labs.

---

## Why This Matters

Without proper Intune tenant preparation:
- Devices cannot be enrolled correctly
- MDM and MAM behavior is inconsistent
- Security policies may not apply as expected
- Troubleshooting becomes difficult

A correctly prepared Intune tenant is essential for **reliable device and endpoint security**.

---

## What Was Implemented

During this lab, the following configurations were performed:

- Microsoft Intune service activation
- Configuration of MDM authority
- Definition of MDM and MAM user scopes
- Validation of Intune tenant readiness
- Initial verification of device management capabilities

No devices were enrolled yet; the focus was on **tenant-level readiness**.

---

## Key Concepts Applied

- Device management architecture
- MDM vs MAM scope understanding
- Tenant-wide configuration impact
- Foundation for scalable device management

---

## Result

At the end of this lab:

- The Intune tenant is fully operational
- Device management scopes are correctly defined
- The environment is ready for device enrollment
- A stable foundation is in place for endpoint security

This lab enables all **device identity and security scenarios** in Phase 3.
