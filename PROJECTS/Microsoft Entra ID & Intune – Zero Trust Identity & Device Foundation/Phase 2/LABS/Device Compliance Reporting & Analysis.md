# LAB 2.2 – Device Compliance Reporting & Analysis

## Goal

The goal of this lab is to **analyze and interpret device compliance reports** in Microsoft Intune in order to understand **why a device is compliant or non-compliant**.

This lab focuses on visibility and troubleshooting, not on creating policies.

---

## Why This Matters

Conditional Access decisions depend heavily on device compliance status.

Without proper reporting and analysis:
- It is unclear why access is allowed or blocked
- Troubleshooting becomes guesswork
- Security teams cannot explain access decisions to users or management

Being able to **read and interpret compliance reports** is a core skill for a Modern Workplace / Cloud Engineer.

---

## What Was Implemented

During this lab, the following actions were performed:

- Review of device compliance status in Intune
- Analysis of compliant vs non-compliant devices
- Identification of specific compliance rules causing non-compliance
- Correlation between compliance policy settings and device state

No new policies were created in this lab; the focus was on **analysis and understanding**.

---

## Validation

The reporting and analysis were validated by:

- Inspecting individual device compliance details
- Reviewing which settings passed or failed
- Confirming that non-compliant devices are clearly flagged
- Understanding how compliance results would impact Conditional Access

---

## Key Concepts Applied

- Compliance visibility and reporting
- Root-cause analysis of non-compliance
- Device state interpretation
- Explainable Zero Trust decisions

---

## Result

At the end of this lab:

- Compliance reports can be correctly interpreted
- Reasons for non-compliance are clearly understood
- Device trust decisions are transparent and explainable
- The environment is ready for Conditional Access enforcement based on compliance

This lab ensures that **Conditional Access decisions can be monitored, validated, and explained**.
